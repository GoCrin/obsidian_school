### 1. Business etiquette in Austria

- **Punctuality:** Keep strictly to the agreed meeting time, and let the others know beforehand if you'll be late. Arriving right on time may even be read as being late, and cancelling at the last minute is extremely rude. Aim to arrive about five minutes early.
