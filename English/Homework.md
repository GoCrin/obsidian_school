**1. Cultural awareness – what & why**

- Understanding differences in values, communication, work habits
- Respecting different worldviews
- Important because: globalized workplaces, avoids misunderstandings, builds trust, better collaboration, core professional skill

**2. Benefits of diversity (workplace)**

- Creativity & innovation (out-of-the-box thinking)
- New perspectives, better problem-solving
- Better products/services (skills from int'l workers)
- Access to new business opportunities
- Higher productivity (varied knowledge/methods)
- Happier, more productive organizations (studies)

**3. Challenges/downsides**

- Communication: language barriers, accents, word choice
- Non-verbal: gestures, eye contact, greetings/handshakes → misunderstandings
- Different work attitudes/norms: dress, behavior, hours (e.g. Japan – long hours/staying late vs. Sweden – ~6h workday)
- Can cause friction/negative feelings

**4. Conclusion (transcript)**

- Diversity = positive **if** challenges are known & managed

**5. Ways to build cross-cultural skills**

- Cultural training/workshops
- Language learning
- International experience (study/work/travel abroad)
- Mentoring/buddy systems
- Active listening, open-mindedness
- Reading/research on other cultures
- Self-reflection on own biases
- Practice in diverse/mixed teams
