# I. World of work
## 1. Job satisfaction
(Article "Motivating high calibre staff")

- **High performer:** people with limitless energy, enthusiasm, very creative, can develop strategies, full of ideas, inspire others, get things done quick and effectively, push organisation to greater heights, qualities shine through even on bad days, -> they are constantly performing on a high level, direct their own learning, keep themselves up to date, ability to prioritize -> efficiency, effective problem-solving, working well in teams, organisational awareness, proactivity, ability to influence, learning agility, technically savvy
- **Outline the problems of losing high performers:** Very attractive to rival companies, loss of productivity, expensive training of other / new employees, huge financial impact, time-consuming to train new employees, inspiration and motivation of remaining employees gets lost, they take their knowledge and expertise with them, difficult to replace, taking clients with them, can lead to delays and other problems
- **inform about factors that help retain high performers:** have to be empowered, e.g. to "own" a project in a creative sense, opportunities to acquire or develop (new) skills, challenging tasks, the company needs to have a clear vision of where they want to go, platforms for self-development, offering time for regeneration and creative thinking, work needs to be varied, want to be seen as special, recognition, expect the company to fast-track their development, want to be supported, good administrative support, carrer growth, job security
